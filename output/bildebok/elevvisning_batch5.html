Syklister: sykkelfelt, sykkelvei og fortau

Når du svinger, har du vikeplikt for syklende som skal rett fram.

  • Sykkelfelt er et kjørefelt som ved skilt og oppmerking er bestemt for syklende.

  • Kommer du fra sykkelvei, gangvei eller fortau og vil inn på veien, har du vikeplikt for trafikanten på veien.

  • Parking: any leaving of a vehicle, even if the driver does not leave it.
  • The shortest possible stop for getting on or off, or loading or unloading, is not parking.
  • If you wait in the car where parking is forbidden, it is illegal parking.

Stopping forbidden: places a–d

Don't stop in a blind bend, a junction, on a pavement or a crossing.

  • A blind bend, tunnel, hilltop or other place with poor view.
  • In a junction or nearer than 5 metres from the junction.
  • Wholly or partly on a pavement, footpath or cycle path. On a pedestrian crossing or cycle crossing, or nearer than 5 metres in front.

Stopping forbidden (e–h) and parking forbidden

No stopping on motorways or in bus lanes. No parking at driveways.

  • Stopping is forbidden on a motorway, nearer than 5 metres to a level crossing and in bus, shared-use and cycle lanes.
  • Parking is forbidden in front of an entrance or exit, in a passing place, on a pedestrian street and on a shared street except at designated places.
  • Near a stop for buses, taxis or trams, special bans apply.

The 5-metre rules

5 metres applies from a junction and in front of crossings.

  • From a junction the 5 metres are measured from where the pavement edge, edge line or road edge begins to curve.
  • It is forbidden to stop on a pedestrian crossing or cycle crossing, or nearer than 5 metres in front of such places.
  • The drawings in the book show 5 m from the junction and in front of pedestrian and cycle crossings.

A stop for buses, taxis and trams

At a stop you may let people off if it does not hinder buses.

  • Stopping is forbidden on the road widening for a stop, or nearer than 20 metres from its sign.
  • Excepted is getting on or off when it does not hinder a bus, taxi or tram.
  • Loading and unloading is not allowed there.

Parking on a priority road: above 50 km/h

On a priority road above 50 km/h, parking on the carriageway is forbidden.

  • The ban applies when the speed limit is higher than 50 km/h.
  • Parking is any leaving of a vehicle, also if the driver stays in the car.
  • Always be alert and careful, also when you park.
