TITLE: Chapter 8 — Traffic signs and road markings

Traffic signs are divided into groups based on shape, colour and function. Road markings give important information about the carriageway.

Sign design and groups:

  • Danger signs — warn of a particular hazard. Triangular shape with a red border. May have a yellow background at road works.
  • Give-way and priority signs — regulate the right-of-way conditions on the stretch of road or place in question.
  • Prohibitory signs — indicate a prohibition on the stretch of road or place in question. Apply in the direction of travel from where the sign is placed up to the nearest road junction.
  • Mandatory signs — indicate that an obligation applies on the stretch of road or place in question. Round shape, blue background.
  • Information signs — indicate that special rules apply or cease to apply.
  • Service signs — give information about emergency help, road services and attractions.
  • Direction signs — give information about place names, businesses, road routes and distances.
  • Supplementary signs — valid only together with a main sign. Give further clarification of the main sign's meaning.
  • Marker signs — give information about the road's further course or about obstacles.

Colours on direction signs:

  • Blue background, white text — motorways
  • Yellow background, black text — other roads
  • White background, black text — other destinations
  • Orange background, black text — temporary direction signing
  • Brown background, white text — tourist traffic

Road markings — longitudinal lines:

  • White centre line — used where there are no special restrictions
  • Yellow centre line — does not separate lanes going in the same direction, but separates opposite directions of travel
  • Warning line — broken line warning of an upcoming barrier line. Overtaking is permitted, but be careful
  • Barrier line — solid line. Forbidden to cross or drive on this line
  • Lane line — separates lanes going in the same direction
Tip: Memory aid for barrier line vs warning line: Barrier line (sperrelinje) = Stop = Solid line. If you see a broken line whose dashes get closer and closer together, it is a warning line announcing an upcoming barrier line.
