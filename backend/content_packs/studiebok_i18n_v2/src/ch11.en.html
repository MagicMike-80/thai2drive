TITLE: Chapter 11 — Insurance, registration and liability

As a car owner and driver you have legal and financial responsibilities you need to know.

The Act on liability for damage caused by motor vehicles (bilansvarslova): Regulates who must pay compensation when a motor vehicle is involved in an accident.

Two types of insurance:

  • Traffic insurance (liability insurance) — Required by law! Covers damage to others (people and vehicles). You cannot register or drive the car without it.
  • Comprehensive insurance (kasko) — Voluntary. Covers damage to your own vehicle.
Road Traffic Act § 15. Registration of motor vehicles:
  • Motor vehicles must be registered.
  • When a motor vehicle changes owner, both parties must notify the registration authority in writing within 3 days.
  • The owner of a motor vehicle must report a change of address within 3 days.
  • Registration cannot take place without an insurance certificate.
Road Traffic Act § 17. Use of motor vehicles: A motor vehicle must not be used unless it has been registered, fitted with legal number plates and issued a vehicle registration card. The registration card must always be carried during use. The owner must make sure that anyone he lets use the motor vehicle meets the requirements for driving it.
Road Traffic Act § 23. Responsibility for the vehicle's condition: Before the drive begins, the driver must make sure that the vehicle is in a safe and regulation-compliant condition and that it is safely and correctly loaded. The owner must ensure that the vehicle is not used if it is not in a safe condition.
Road Traffic Act § 12. Duties at a traffic accident:
  • Anyone involved in a traffic accident must stop immediately and help injured people and animals.
  • Those involved have a mutual duty to give their name and address.
  • If a traffic accident has caused death or serious injury, the police must be notified as soon as possible.
  • Vehicles that are a danger to traffic must be moved immediately.
  • Traces must not be removed and other matters of importance to the investigation must not be changed.
Tip: Documents you must carry while driving: driving licence (always), vehicle registration card (always), and any certificates of competence. You do not need to carry the insurance certificate — it is registered digitally.
