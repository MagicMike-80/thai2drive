TITLE: Chapter 7 — Stopping and parking

The rules on stopping and parking are important for road safety and traffic flow.

Traffic Rules § 17. Stopping and parking:

It is forbidden to stop:

  • In a blind curve, in a tunnel, on a hilltop or in any other place with poor visibility
  • In a road junction or closer than 5 metres from the junction
  • Fully or partly on a pavement, footpath or cycle path
  • On a pedestrian crossing or cycle crossing, or closer than 5 metres before such places
  • On motorways and motor traffic roads
  • Closer than 5 metres to a level crossing
  • In a public transport lane, carpool lane or cycle lane (exception: bus/tram at a stop)
  • Closer than 20 metres from an official traffic sign for a bus/tram stop (exception: picking up/dropping off passengers if it does not obstruct the bus/taxi/tram)

It is forbidden to park:

  • In front of an entrance or exit (driveway)
  • On a passing place, in the road's full width
  • In a pedestrian street
  • In a home zone (gatetun) outside specially marked spaces
  • On a priority road with a speed limit above 50 km/h: parking on the carriageway is forbidden
Road Traffic Act § 8. Parking: The King may issue regulations on parking fees and on prohibiting parking unless the fee has been paid in advance. The municipality may reserve parking spaces for specific groups.
Tip — Memory aid for the 5-metre rule: Remember that the 5-metre distance is measured from where the kerb begins to curve towards the junction — not from the junction point itself. It is often further than people think!
Traffic Rules § 15. Use of lights: Parking lights must be on while stopped or parked on a road when light or visibility conditions require it to make the vehicle visible. High beam or low beam must not be on during such stopping/parking.
