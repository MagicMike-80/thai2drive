TITLE: Chapter 12 — Driving licence, practice driving and training

To drive a car in Norway you need a driving licence. Here are the most important rules for training and practice driving.

Requirements for a class B driving licence (passenger car):

  • At least 18 years old
  • Passed the theory test and the practical driving test
  • Meet the health requirements (vision, hearing, mobility)
  • Sober habits and good conduct
  • Completed the mandatory training

Requirements for private practice driving:

Requirements for the learner:

  • At least 16 years old
  • No disqualification period (sperrefrist)
  • Be fit to drive
  • Completed the basic traffic course (trafikalt grunnkurs)

Requirements for the accompanying person:

  • At least 25 years old
  • Held a valid driving licence continuously for the last 5 years
  • Be fit to act as an accompanying person
Road Traffic Act § 26. Practice driving: Practice driving must not be a danger or unnecessary inconvenience to other traffic. Practice driving with a motor vehicle must not take place earlier than 2 years before the time when the learner, by age, can obtain the right to drive. During practice driving, the instructor or accompanying person is considered the driver of the motor vehicle — but the rules on alcohol and drugs also apply to the learner.

The step-by-step training for class B:

  • Step 1: Basic traffic course — basic traffic behaviour and understanding
  • Step 2: Basic vehicle and driving competence — technical driving, safety checks
  • Step 3: Traffic training — safe driving in varied traffic, safety course on a closed track
  • Step 4: Final training — rural road driving, overtaking, safety course in traffic
Goal of the training (§ 8-2): Through experience, activity and reflection, the learner shall become aware of what it means that everyone must travel considerately and be alert and careful so that no danger can arise and no harm be caused.
Tip: During the first nine months with a driving licence, a new driver has a 40 times higher risk of running off the road than an experienced driver — mainly due to lack of experience and incorrect speed adaptation. Take it easy during the first months!
