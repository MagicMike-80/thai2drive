TITLE: Chapter 6 — Overtaking, reversing and turning around

Overtaking is one of the most dangerous manoeuvres in traffic. It is important to know the rules thoroughly.

Traffic Rules § 12. Overtaking:
  1. Overtaking must be done on the left. Exceptions: the vehicle in front is turning left (then you pass on the right); trams may be overtaken on the right; dense queues where all lanes are occupied.
  2. Before overtaking, the driver must make sure that:
    • The road is free of obstacles for a sufficiently long stretch ahead
    • The vehicle in front has not signalled an overtaking manoeuvre
    • No vehicle behind has started to overtake
    • It is possible to re-enter the traffic flow without disturbing it
  3. After overtaking: move back to the right without endangering the vehicle you passed.
  4. The vehicle being overtaken must keep to the right and not increase speed during the overtaking.
  5. Overtaking is forbidden: just before or in a road junction (with exceptions), where visibility is restricted by a hilltop, curve or in any other way, and near pedestrian crossings (do not overtake a vehicle that blocks your view of the crossing).
Traffic Rules § 11. Reversing and turning around:
  • Anyone reversing or turning around must give way to other road users.
  • If visibility is not sufficient, reversing/turning must not be done unless someone else keeps watch.
  • Reversing and turning around are forbidden on motorways and motor traffic roads.
Tip: Overtaking requires a LARGE safety margin. Remember: you need room for acceleration, passing and merging back in — and oncoming traffic may approach at high speed. When in doubt — wait!
