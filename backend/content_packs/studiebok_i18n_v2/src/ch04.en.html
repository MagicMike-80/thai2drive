TITLE: Chapter 4 — Speed and speed limits

Correct speed is crucial for road safety. Section 6 of the Road Traffic Act and Section 13 of the Traffic Rules regulate driving speed.

Road Traffic Act § 6. Speed rules: The driver of a vehicle must adjust the speed to the location, road, visibility and traffic conditions so that no danger can arise and no inconvenience is caused to others. The driver must always have full control of the vehicle.

General speed limits (without signs):

  • In built-up areas: 50 km/h
  • Outside built-up areas: 80 km/h
Traffic Rules § 13. Special provisions on driving speed:
  • Drivers must be able to stop within the stretch of road they can see ahead, and in front of any foreseeable obstacle.
  • You must keep a sufficiently low speed and stop when passing: children on the road, a school patrol, a person with a white cane or guide dog, a tram/bus at a stop.
  • In pedestrian streets or home zones (gatetun): no faster than walking pace
  • Motor vehicles over 3,500 kg or with a trailer: no faster than 80 km/h where the speed limit is higher
  • Motor vehicles with an unbraked trailer (trailer 300 kg+): no faster than 60 km/h

Stopping distances — the relationship between speed and stopping distance:

  • 20 km/h: reaction distance approx. 5.5 m + braking distance 2 m = approx. 7.5 m total
  • 50 km/h: reaction distance approx. 14 m + braking distance 12.5 m = approx. 26.5 m total
  • 80 km/h: reaction distance approx. 22 m + braking distance 32 m = approx. 54 m total
  • 100 km/h: reaction distance approx. 27.8 m + braking distance 50 m = approx. 78 m total
Tip: The stopping distance increases with the square of the speed — if you double your speed, you quadruple your braking distance! Reaction time is about 1 second for a normally attentive driver.

Factors that affect the correct speed:

  • Visibility conditions (darkness, fog, rain, snow)
  • Road conditions (ice, snow, wet asphalt)
  • Traffic volume and types of road users
  • Road design (curves, hilltops, junctions)
  • Signed speed limit
