TITLE: Chapter 5 — Right of way and priority roads

Giving way (vikeplikt) means rules that decide who must wait and who may drive. These rules are fundamental for safe traffic flow.

Traffic Rules § 7. Duty to give way:
  1. A road user you must give way to must not be obstructed or disturbed. Anyone who has a duty to give way must show this clearly by slowing down or stopping in good time.
  2. Drivers must give way to vehicles coming from the right (the right-hand rule). The same applies when a driver who wants to turn left will get a vehicle on their right side.
  3. Drivers who want to turn must give way to pedestrians or cyclists where they are turning in.
  4. Drivers coming from a parking lot, bus stop, square, property, petrol station, pedestrian street, home zone or similar area must give way to other road users. The same applies when entering from the shoulder or from a cycle path/footpath/pavement.
  5. On roads with a speed limit of 60 km/h or lower: drivers must give way to a bus leaving a bus stop (when the bus driver signals).
  6. Oncoming road users must move to the right in good time and drive slowly. If part of the road is blocked, the one with the obstruction on their side must give way.
Traffic Rules § 9. Special duties towards pedestrians:
  • Drivers must give pedestrians sufficient space on the road.
  • Drivers crossing a footpath or pavement must give way to pedestrians.
  • At a pedestrian crossing (without lights/police): give way to pedestrians who are in the crossing or about to step into it.
  • Drivers must avoid stopping on a pedestrian crossing.
Traffic Rules § 10. Clear passage:
  • Road users must give a clear passage to emergency vehicles with flashing blue lights.
  • Road users must give a clear passage to trams and trains.
  • Road users must not obstruct pedestrians in a supervised group, a procession, a funeral procession, or a military vehicle convoy.
Tip — Memory aid for giving way: Think HFGP: Høyre (the right-hand rule), Forkjørsveg (priority road signs), Gangfelt (give way to pedestrians), Parkering/innkjøring (always give way when entering from parking/driveways).

Order of priority at junctions:

  • Police instructions
  • Traffic light signals
  • Give way/priority signs
  • The right-hand rule (when none of the above apply)
