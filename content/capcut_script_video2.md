## 🎵 Bakgrunnsmusikk

**Musikk-valg (Folk/acoustic vibe for Facebook — ikkje aggressiv):**
- **Segment 1:** Uplifting acoustic gitarmelodi, medium tempo (100–110 BPM) → **Genre:** Folk, warm, inspirational
  - *Eksempel:* "Bright Horizons" eller "Folk Journey"
- **Segment 2:** Same gitarmelodi, slight crescendo (volum +20%) → **Genre:** Folk with building tension
  - *Eksempel:* "Growing Hope"
- **Segment 3:** Stronger gitarmelodi + light percussion (drums, maraca-stil), energi-boost → **Genre:** Uplifting folk, energetic
  - *Eksempel:* "Success Heartbeat"
- **Segment 4:** Full crescendo, triumfant gitarmelodi + light strings/percussion → **Genre:** Triumphant, celebratory
  - *Eksempel:* "Victory Dance"

**Volum-kurve:**
- Segment 1: 50% volum
- Segment 2: 55% volum
- Segment 3: 65% volum
- Segment 4: 75% volum (full energi)
