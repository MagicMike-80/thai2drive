## 🎵 Bakgrunnsmusikk

**Musikk-valg (Educational + drama, safety-fokusert):**
- **Segment 1:** Intense, dramatic sting (alarm/attention), minor key → **Genre:** Dramatic alert, attention-grabbing
  - *Eksempel:* "Safety Alert" eller "Critical Moment"
- **Segment 2:** Educational, steady, tempo slows → **Genre:** Informative, clinical, serious
  - *Eksempel:* "Learning Mode" eller "Educational Focus"
- **Segment 3:** Dramatic, tense, tempo picks up → **Genre:** Danger/urgency, building concern
  - *Eksempel:* "Rising Tension" eller "Critical Risk"
- **Segment 4:** Major key, hopeful, safe-feeling → **Genre:** Resolution, safety, trust
  - *Eksempel:* "Safe Haven" eller "Confidence Restored"

**Volum-kurve:**
- Segment 1: 50% volum (dramatic hit)
- Segment 2: 55% volum (educational, steady)
- Segment 3: 65% volum (building concern)
- Segment 4: 70% volum (resolution, confident)

## 🎤 Voiceover-manus

### NORSK (mannsstemme, seriøs, educational, ikkje skremande men ernst)

**Segment 1 (0:00–0:08):**
> "50 kilometer per time i Thailand — sikker. I Norge — feil. Farleg."

*Tone:* Direct, stark statement. Serious but not alarmist.
*Pacing:* Slow, pauses etter "Thailand" og "Norge" for impact.
*Volum:* Fullt volum frå start.

**Segment 2 (0:08–0:24):**
> "Ved 50 kilometer: reaktidslengde, 14 meter. Bremselengde, 27 meter. Stoppelengde totalt — 41 meter. Det er meir enn ein liten hus-lengde."

*Tone:* Educational, measured. Talking thru numbers calmly, then context (bigger than a house).
*Pacing:* Medium-slow, natural pauses between numbers.
*Volum:* Fullt volum.

**Segment 3 (0:24–0:35):**
> "Du må læra NORSK fart, NORSK bremsing, NORSK avstandar. Ikkje Thailand-tanken. Michael viser deg tala som betyr liv eller død i Noreg."

*Tone:* Emphatic, urgent but controlled. "Life or death" is serious but validated by numbers.
*Pacing:* Faster, with emphasis on repeated "NORSK."
*Volum:* Fullt volum, emphase på NORSK/tala/liv-eller-død.

**Segment 4 (0:35–0:45):**
> "Kjør som ein norsk sjåfør. Kjør trygt."

*Tone:* Reassuring, motivational. "You can do this if you learn."
*Pacing:* Normal tempo, with warmth.
*Volum:* Fullt volum.
