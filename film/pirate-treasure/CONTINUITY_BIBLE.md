# The Lantern of the Sands — Continuity Bible

Reference sheet for the 12 prompts in `AI_VIDEO_PROMPTS.md`. The prompt file itself
contains only the Final Combined AI Video Prompts, one continuous paragraph per scene,
separated by `---`, in strict story order. This file exists so the same characters,
props and vehicles can be locked into reference images before generation.

## Source

Generated from the supplied film idea (pirates unearth a glowing stone; the light wakes a
herd of giant ostriches; a desert chase to the flying vehicles). No full script and no
scene reference images were supplied, so the 12-scene breakdown and all dialogue below
were authored to cover the story beat-for-beat. If real reference images or a locked
script arrive, the prompts should be regenerated against them.

## Props and vehicles

- **The Lantern of the Sands** — smooth fist-sized translucent obsidian-and-amber stone veined with living golden-blue filaments that pulse like a slow heartbeat.
- **Giant ostriches** — four metres tall, dust-dulled black and grey plumage, scaled two-toed legs; eyes burn golden-blue while the stone is exposed and fade to natural black-brown when it is buried.
- **Sky skiffs** — four fifteen-metre vessels, dark tarred timber with exposed brass ribbing, tattered crimson sails, harpoon rigging, three humming copper anti-gravity rings under each hull glowing turquoise.

## Scene order

1. Dawn dig — nine pirates, eleven hours in, nothing found
2. The shovel strikes — buried glow uncovered by hand
3. The lift — light detonates across the whole desert
4. Two kilometres away — the herd wakes, eyes ignite
5. Back at the pit — the ground begins to shake
6. The ridge — hundreds of ostriches crest and charge
7. The sprint across open sand
8. Reaching the moored sky skiffs, cutting anchor
9. Violent liftoff, lead ostrich seizes the anchor chain
10. Aerial chase through the dune canyon
11. The Captain releases the stone
12. The herd stands down, the fleet sails into the sunrise
